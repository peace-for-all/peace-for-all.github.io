ECOWATCH(1) · case file · prototype

EcoWatch

Field observations should survive patchy networks, tired hands, and the interval between noticing something and returning to a desk.

shape
Android · offline-first prototype
constraint
no dependable connection

field-conditions

Paper, photos, and memory can require reconstruction later. A connected form may look tidy in an office and fail at the moment an observation matters.

offline-contract

Local capture is the primary path, not a degraded fallback. A saved observation must remain reviewable and editable without a network.

observe → local draft → verify/edit → later transfer
captured fact ≠ later interpretation

capture

The prototype demonstrates local observation capture, persistence, and review. Coordinates are useful evidence but not infallible truth.

[trace 4/4] inspect coordinate field

Coordinates remain editable because GPS can be confidently wrong.

sync-assumptions

Transfer must expose pending and failed state. Conflicts and provenance need comprehensible rules before synchronization can be called reliable.

unresolved

Multi-device synchronization, operational security, long-term data governance, and real-program use remain unresolved and require work with practitioners. This prototype makes no environmental outcome claim and has no public build or source link.
