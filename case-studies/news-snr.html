NEWS-SNR(1) · research pilot · local data pipeline

Public Discourse Observatory

A research pilot for studying public discourse without pretending that a single sentiment number, a polished dashboard, or a large language model can tell the truth on its own.

stack
Python 3.11 · SQLite · explainable local analysis · web dashboard · browser-capture companion
boundary
live collection stays blocked until governance and pseudonymization are satisfied

the signal is messier than the chart

Public reaction data is easy to turn into a misleading aggregate: emoji meanings are ambiguous, venues have different audiences, and a missing source can look like silence. The pilot separates what was observed from what can be claimed.

raw observation → versioned annotation → narrow claim
missing coverage is never converted into quiet or closure

immutable raw data, versioned derived data

Raw observations are stored immutably. Derived annotations and analysis records are versioned separately, so a later taxonomy or method change does not rewrite history. Events, venues, sources, and phases stay distinct instead of being pooled.

observations are append-only
derived records carry their own version and provenance

explainable similarity and bounded statistics

Similarity prefers comparable events and returns the components of a match rather than one opaque score. Engagement uses bounded intervals such as Wilson, and stance/framing analysis is multilabel with matched evidence. The system also returns rival hypotheses and an explicit claim boundary.

comparable-event matching · matched terms · bounded engagement intervals
a result comes with “why” and “what this does not establish”

collection is gated by design

Live scanning remains blocked until a named researcher supplies a completed feasibility record and pseudonymization secret. The browser-capture path is explicit, does not retain account identity or cookies, and stores only the visible post. Retention and deletion rules are part of the study governance, not an afterthought.

preflight → approved sources/terms → pseudonym key → bounded live scan
the default request identity is the honest, contact-free news-snr/0.2

status

The included fixture is synthetic and exists only to exercise the system; it is not research evidence. This is a runnable research foundation, not a claim that a particular public event was measured or resolved. The value is the pipeline and its explicit limits.
