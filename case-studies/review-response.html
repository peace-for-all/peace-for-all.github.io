Case study · Production internal software

Anonymized review-response system

A working team needed a steadier way to prepare public replies while keeping a person responsible for every response.

For
An operational team
Shape
Internal production system
Boundary
Human review before use

The nuisance

Incoming reviews vary in tone and context, yet each deserves a timely, specific response. Repetition consumes attention; hurried templates can sound indifferent and miss details that require care.

What became clear

The initial automation opportunity looked like text generation. The awkward discovery was that drafting was only one step. Context selection, exceptions, review, failure visibility, and a clear handoff to a responsible person were the real system.

Decisions

The software separates marketplace ingestion, draft preparation, operator review, and release. Language-model assistance is bounded as a drafting tool. Deterministic rules, versioned business instructions, validation, dry-run controls, and explicit write gates surround the parts that can affect a customer.

The operator dashboard makes queue state, draft provenance, safety findings, retries, and failures visible. Unattended paths fail closed; unusual or negative cases remain with people rather than being forced through a happy-path template.

What works now

The internal system operates in production. It ingests marketplace reviews and questions, routes work to operators, prepares constrained drafts, supports review and correction, and posts only through controlled paths. Health checks, audit records, and emergency pauses make operational behaviour inspectable.

Status and limits

It remains internal software tied to a particular workflow. Generated text can be wrong, tonally unsuitable, or insensitive to context; human approval and conservative automation gates are product requirements, not temporary safeguards. No unsupported performance or business-outcome figures are claimed here.

Technology, where it matters

Python, a lightweight operator dashboard, marketplace APIs, Telegram routing, persistent audit state, and LLM/vision services form a maintainable pipeline around human judgment. The important architecture is not the model call; it is the observable, reversible system around it. Vendor, client, prompts, credentials, volumes, screenshots, and infrastructure details are intentionally omitted.
