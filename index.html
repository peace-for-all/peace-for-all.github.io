More than one kind of engineer.

How I work

I begin with the nuisance in its real setting: who meets it, what they are trying to do, and which constraints will still be there after the novelty wears off. I prefer short feedback loops, candid trade-offs, small releases, inspectable systems, and plain documentation.

I take reliability and user trust seriously; I try not to take ceremony or ego too seriously. When something fails, I prefer a calm diagnosis and a durable fix to a dramatic meeting.

LLMs assist with exploration, implementation, review, testing, and documentation. I remain responsible for product judgment, verification, security, and maintenance. Generated work is a draft to examine, not authority to outsource.

We may work well together if…

You have a useful problem rather than a predetermined feature list; you value candid discussion of trade-offs; and you want software that can be understood and cared for after launch. I especially enjoy teams of sharp, kind people who take the challenge seriously without taking themselves too seriously.

I’m open to small paid projects, ongoing contracts, and employment where the work crosses boundaries and ownership matters.
