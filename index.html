How I work

I begin with the nuisance in its real setting: who meets it, what they are trying to do, and which constraints will still be there after the novelty wears off. I prefer small releases, inspectable systems, and plain documentation.

LLMs assist with exploration, implementation, review, testing, and documentation. I remain responsible for the product judgment, verification, security, and maintenance. Generated work is a draft to examine, not authority to outsource.

We may work well together if…

You have a useful problem rather than a predetermined feature list; you value candid discussion of trade-offs; and you want software that can be understood and cared for after launch. I’m open to small paid projects, ongoing contracts, and employment with thoughtful, attentive teams.
