VoiceGuides privacy policy

VoiceGuides is designed to work locally. The app developer does not operate a service that collects, receives, or sells your app data.

Data the app uses

VoiceGuides processes the Markdown and plain-text files you choose through Android’s system folder picker or share/open with the app. It stores listening position, app settings, notes, explorations, completion reviews, and private offline copies on your device. Parsed narration may also be cached locally for faster playback.

The app uses the text-to-speech engine installed and selected on your Android device. That engine is separate software with its own behavior and privacy terms. VoiceGuides itself does not request internet or microphone permission.

File access

When you choose a folder, Android grants the app access to that location. VoiceGuides scans supported files recursively and treats the source folder as read-only. You can revoke access through Android settings, remove the folder permission, or uninstall the app. A file explicitly opened or shared with VoiceGuides may be copied into the app’s private storage so it remains available for listening.

Sharing and backups

Sharing occurs only after you choose a copy, share, backup, or export action. VoiceGuides then hands prepared text, a Markdown file, or a JSON backup to Android’s system sharing interface or document workflow. The destination app or service you select processes that data under its own privacy policy.

Backups can contain listening progress, notes, explorations, and related app data. You decide where to save them and are responsible for protecting and deleting copies outside VoiceGuides. Import reads a backup file that you select.

Retention and deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete individual content in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it, subject to Android’s own backup and restore settings. Changing or removing a source folder does not necessarily remove private offline copies, notes, or backups you exported. Files shared to another app must be deleted from that destination separately.

Collection, analytics, and advertising

The developer does not receive app content or usage data. VoiceGuides has no developer-operated account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or remote server. It does not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Security and limitations

Local-first storage reduces network exposure but cannot protect an unlocked or compromised device. Use the device security appropriate for the sensitivity of your files and notes. Android, your TTS engine, keyboard, backup provider, and apps you share with are outside VoiceGuides and may process data independently.

Children

VoiceGuides is a general-purpose reading tool and is not directed to children. Because the developer operates no collection service, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions or deletion concerns about developer-held data may be sent to walsk@yandex.com; because the app does not transmit data to the developer, there is normally no server-side account or app content for the developer to delete.
